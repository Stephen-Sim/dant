## Unreleased automatic continuation of unfinished work

The Worker now checks saved unfinished tasks every 30 seconds and coalesces a check about five seconds after progress changes. It continues one eligible task in the existing conversation, preserving its work ID, source, priority and turn history without adding a fake human message or replaying attachments. Human input and the normal queue take precedence. A fresh executor read must confirm the previous turn ended and the shared thread is idle before submission. Actual waiting, pause, cancellation, review, human control and uncertain outcomes remain excluded. Three automatic rounds without a changed public-plan checkpoint pause the task with a visible reason.

Isolated regression fixtures cover durable claims and failed saves, restart with uncertain ownership, priority and delayed checks, actual completion and clarification, disconnection, pause and takeover, ordinary human input, early terminal events, acknowledged binding failures, and live-call admission races. Native handoff receipts and queued-but-unprocessed turn events prevent an automatic binding; uncertain acknowledgements never authorize interrupting a possible native owner. The final admission fence and durable binding are synchronous. The public Codex `turn/start` may start or steer, so this implementation does not claim an upstream atomic start-if-idle guarantee. This is source-only validation; no installed app, VM work, live model or audio session was changed.
